# LAURA v1.7 — Cyclic sugar chair lab
Run Run LAURA.bat (port 8770), then choose Chair lab.
Explore alpha/beta D-glucopyranose, D-mannopyranose and D-galactopyranose. Compare the Haworth projection with an idealized rotatable 3D chair and a substituent-position table. Flip between 4C1 and 1C4. Up/down orientations and anomer identity stay fixed during a flip; axial/equatorial positions exchange.

The 3D structure is a schematic with labeled group endpoints, not an all-atom or energy-minimized molecule. Hydrogens are omitted. No transition pathway, ring closure reaction, energy, or solution populations are modeled. This lab uses predefined cyclic sugars rather than detecting rings in imported coordinates.
Reference: https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/25-5-cyclic-structures-of-monosaccharides-anomers
